Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Flash Fizz ("the App") is developed by KindKoda ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We operate no servers and no accounts. All game progress, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device only.

The App can open your device's native share sheet so you can share a challenge code with someone else. What you share, and with whom, is entirely your choice. The App does not read your contacts, your messages, or anything from the app you share into.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services (Android) / Apple Game Center (iOS): If you are signed in on your device, the App may use the platform's gaming service to show your player name and submit scores to a leaderboard. This sign-in is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; the information is not sent to us and we do not store it. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This App is designed to be suitable for all ages, including children under 13, and is configured for child-directed treatment. In this configuration:

  • All ad requests are tagged as child-directed
  • Personalized advertising is disabled - ads are requested as non-personalized
  • Ad content is restricted to age-appropriate material (rated G)
  • No personal information from children is knowingly collected

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can delete it.

4. Advertising

The App displays banner and interstitial advertisements and offers optional rewarded video ads. These ads are provided by Google AdMob and are requested as non-personalized. You can also manage ad personalization through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

5. Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using the platform's standard app preferences storage (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS). This includes your high score, current level, session count, crystal balance, upgrade and prestige levels, daily streak, unlocked items, sound and haptic settings, and whether you have dismissed the rating prompt. No data is transmitted to external servers by the App. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

6. Permissions

The App requires the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to display advertisements
  • Network State: Required to check connectivity for ad loading

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
