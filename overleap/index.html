Installing it

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will warn you that it came from an unknown source and offer a settings toggle. That warning appears for every app not installed from the Play Store, which is all this is.
  3. Allow it, tap Install, and play.

The game asks for no permissions at all, not even internet, so there is nothing further to grant. You can check that yourself under Settings > Apps > Overleap > Permissions.

Playing it

  • Tap to leap.
  • Hold to keep bouncing off every surface you touch.
  • Tilt the phone to steer. No tilt? Hold a side of the screen instead.
  • Chain leaps without touching the ground to build a multiplier, and keep ahead of the Undertow rising below you.

What is in it

  • Four classes, each with one signature ability
  • Nine zones with their own painted art
  • 53 achievement tiers across 9 tracks
  • Race a friend: share a code and you both climb the identical tower
  • No ads, no purchases, no accounts, and it works with the phone in aeroplane mode
  • Your progress rides Android's own backup to your next phone, in your Google account, where we cannot see it
