Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Overleap ("the App") is developed by KindKoda ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our App.

In short: the App collects nothing. It requests no Android permissions at all, it has no internet access, and it contains no advertising, no analytics and no accounts. Everything it saves stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit or have access to any personal information. We cannot see your scores, your settings or anything else about your use of the App.

2. Permissions

The App declares no Android permissions whatsoever. In particular it does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending data anywhere. You can verify this yourself: on your device, open Settings > Apps > Overleap > Permissions.

3. Data Stored On Your Device

So the game can remember your progress between sessions, the following is saved locally on your device only, in the App's private storage:

  • Best score, best floor, and total number of climbs
  • Which characters and achievements you have unlocked
  • Your settings, such as audio volume and graphics quality
  • Replay data for your own past climbs, used to race your previous runs

This information never leaves your device. It is not backed up to us, it is not shared, and it is not readable by us or by anyone else. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of it.

4. Third-Party Services

None. The App contains no advertising networks, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social media integrations and no third-party software development kits of any kind.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. It complies with COPPA and with the Google Play Families policy by design rather than by configuration.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information, because we do not have any.

7. Your Rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose or erase. To delete the data stored locally on your device, uninstall the App or clear its storage from your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. If the App ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
